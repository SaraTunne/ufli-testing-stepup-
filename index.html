Administration Script

Read this to the student before beginning the placement test:

“Today, we are going to take a test to see where you are at in reading.

I’m going to show you some words to read and listen to. Some are real words and some are made-up words that may or may not be tricky to read, but please try your best. Remember that some are made-up words, so do not try to make them sound like real words. Ready?”
Instructions:
  • Words in every set are interleaved into a mixed sequence.
  • The student can click Listen 🔊 to hear the word read phonetically.
  • The student selects Real Word 🟢 or Made-Up Word 🟣.
  • Each correct classification earns 1 point.
  • At the end of each set, the score breakdown is displayed.
